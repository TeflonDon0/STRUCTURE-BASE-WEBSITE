SB / Privacy

How your information is handled.

{{ site_settings.site_name }} uses information submitted through this site to publish listings, respond to enquiries, manage maintenance requests, and administer partner applications and referrals.

Listing Data

Property details added through the dashboard are used to build public listing pages whenever a record is published.

Uploaded Images

Published property photos appear with their listings. A photo submitted with a maintenance request is attached to that request for operations follow-up.

Direct Enquiries

Enquiry and inspection forms store your contact details, selected property, message, and requested viewing details so authorised staff can respond and coordinate follow-up. External phone, email, and WhatsApp links open the relevant service when configured.

Maintenance requests

Resident name, unit, contact details, issue description, priority, and any attached photo are stored for the operations team to review and manage the request.

Partner accounts and referrals

Partner applications store identity, contact, location, and marketing-profile details for review. Passwords are stored as hashes. Referral links and recorded activity connect eligible introductions to leads and commission records; partner access is restricted to the records attributed to that account.

Sessions and service providers

Cookies support sign-in, form security, and property-referral attribution. The site also uses external font and map services. Depending on the deployment configuration, database, image-storage, and email providers support processing and delivery.
